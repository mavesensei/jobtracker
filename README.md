## What is it?

Jobourney is a job application tracker built for people who are serious about their search. Instead of a messy spreadsheet or a forgotten notes app, you get a clean kanban board that shows exactly where every application stands — and AI-powered tools to help you figure out what's actually working.

Paste your resume. Let Gemini analyze how well it fits a role. Check your analytics to see which industries and company types respond to you most. Keep your streak alive. Land the job.

---

## Features

- **Application tracking** — status columns for Applied, Interview, Rejected, and Offered, always sorted newest-first
- **LinkedIn auto-fill** — paste a job URL and the key details fill themselves in
- **AI resume analysis** — Gemini scores your resume against any role and shows exactly what's missing
- **Smart analytics** — interview rate broken down by company type and industry so you know where to focus
- **Daily streak** — a small thing that keeps you consistent on tough days
- **Follow-up reminders** — never go dark on an application that still has a shot
- **Mobile responsive** — works on your phone, because job hunting doesn't stop at a desk

---

## How it works

**1. Add an application**
Drop in a company name, role, and LinkedIn URL. The form stays out of your way — quick to fill, quick to submit.

**2. Run a resume fit check**
Paste your resume once. Jobourney uses Gemini to score the fit against the specific role and surfaces the keywords, strengths, and gaps that matter most.

**3. Check your analytics**
After a few applications, the analytics page starts showing patterns — which industries respond, which company types convert, where you're losing momentum. Use that to sharpen your targeting.
